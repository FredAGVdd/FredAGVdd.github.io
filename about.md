I'm Frederik — a senior full-stack engineer based in Malta. I've been building for the web since back when JavaScript was "that little helper language" and Smalltalk quietly ran the world from behind the curtain.

Twenty-five-odd years later I'm still at it: **TypeScript, React and Next.js** up front; **Node.js, Strapi and PostgreSQL** out back. And this past year I took a proper dive into shipping *production* AI features with Claude and friends — moderation guardrails, translation pipelines, multi-agent workflows. It turns out the best use of a couple of decades of scar tissue is knowing which of the model's very confident answers to actually believe.

I like the unglamorous parts — making things fast, correct and maintainable. I like the glamorous parts too, on the good days when they survive contact with production.

> "Electrons are fascinating beyond compare" — a few milliwatts, and yet they carry a signal around the planet at nearly the speed of light. Still the best magic trick I know.

Off the keyboard, you'll usually find me somewhere in the Maltese sun, or taking something apart to figure out how it works so I can go build a small version of it.
